How it works

DREAM-RAW puts its own menu, DC-RAW, on slot 01 of your GDEMU's SD card. When you start a game from it, DC-RAW stays with the game and passes the values the achievement set needs to your PC over a serial cable. The program on the PC checks the achievements and unlocks them on your RetroAchievements account. Your game images are never changed.

The game names itself

When you start a game, DC-RAW tells the PC which disc it is. The program finds the matching RetroAchievements set by itself. Nothing to select.

One click on the SD card

The program finds the GDEMU card, checks your games with RetroAchievements and writes the DC-RAW menu. What was on slot 01 before is backed up, and Restore original menu puts it back.

Your games in one list

DC-RAW lists every game on the card and shows the RetroAchievements icon and status of the one you select. Pick a game and press A.

Unlocks at the edge of the screen

When an achievement unlocks, a card with its badge slides in at the edge of your screen. The program window shows the achievement list and live values from the console's memory.

The cable

The Dreamcast talks to the PC through its serial port. A ready-made cable with a 3.3 V USB-to-serial adapter works, or you build one yourself from two parts and five wires. These are the parts I used — an example, not a requirement. A CP2102N board with RTS and CTS is my recommendation.

Known limits

Games need to be GDI images on the SD card; CDI images, mostly homebrew, can't be started from the DC-RAW menu yet. Games without an achievement set start normally.

Requirements
